Forecast maps

Multi-model average (ECMWF SEAS5 + Météo-France) forecast SST at leads L1 to L6 of the latest forecast (L1 is the month it is issued).1 Precipitation indicators (total, extremes, drought) will be added once the daily forecast fields are processed.

Notes

  1. The field is the variance-adjusted SST anomaly: the concurrent 20°S to 20°N tropical mean is removed and the remainder is standardized by the local observed SST standard deviation (σ units), so it shows how anomalous each region is relative to its own variability rather than how warm the tropics are as a whole. ↑
  2. Each model's average of its runs is computed on its native grid and regridded to a common 1°×1° mesh. Centres are combined with equal weight (one centre = one vote), following a one-centre-one-vote convention. The per-cell value shown is the average of the centres for the selected variable at the chosen lead.
  3. Maps for the current forecast only; past inits are not stored as maps (they're available as time-series on the Hindcasts tab).
