ENSOscope outbreak records that may be archived

outbreak_records.csv holds the outbreaks shown on the event replay timeline that come from
sources whose terms allow them to be archived and passed on. Each row is one outbreak as that
source holds it. Records from other sources stay on the live site only, until their owners
agree. All data sources: https://iadicarlo.org/ensoscope/methodology.html#data-sources

Columns: source, country (ENSOscope id), iso3, disease, start and end month (YYYY-MM),
where (place or report count, as on the site), link (the record or the source).

Sources included, with how to credit them:
- Leptospirosis, leishmaniasis and hantavirus in Brazil: Ministério da Saúde, SINAN, via DATASUS (CC BY)
- WHO Disease Outbreak News: Torres Munguía et al. (2022), Sci. Data 9, 683, doi:10.6084/m9.figshare.17207183 (CC BY 4.0)
